# The Wyrm Hunters --- Visual Identity Guide

## 1. Brand Direction

**Brand:** The Wyrm Hunters\
**Slogan:** We Hunt. You Rest.

**Visual identity:** Premium medieval fantasy, elite monster-hunting
guild, restrained heraldic design.

The website should communicate professionalism, discretion, reliability,
and authority. Its visual language combines deep forest greens, antique
gold, aged metal, and subtle parchment tones.

Avoid generic RPG interface styling, excessive ornamentation, bright
fantasy colors, cartoon-like icons, and overly decorative elements.

### Color Usage Rules

-   Use Deep Forest (`#101B17`) as the primary page background.
-   Use Guild Green (`#1C2B23`) to distinguish alternate sections and
    large content panels.
-   Use Card Green (`#24352B`) for cards and grouped content.
-   Use Antique Gold (`#B49354`) sparingly for important accents,
    primary buttons, and heraldic details.
-   Use Pale Gold (`#D0B77D`) for hover states and selected highlights.
-   Use Old Parchment (`#D8D1BF`) for readable body text on dark
    backgrounds.
-   Use Heading Ivory (`#E4DCC9`) for headings.
-   Use Stone Grey (`#777C75`) and Secondary Text (`#A7A99D`) for
    secondary information.
-   Reserve Blood Burgundy (`#713B32`) for danger, critical status, and
    threat-related elements.
-   Use silver or muted steel only as a supporting material in
    illustrations and icons; it is not a dominant interface color.
-   Avoid large bright or highly saturated color areas.

## 3. Icon System

### General Concept

Create a consistent collection of five premium medieval heraldic emblems
for the website's service categories.

Each icon must have: - A distinct geometric base shape. - One clearly
recognizable central symbol. - A symmetrical, centered composition. - An
engraved antique-metal appearance. - A consistent dark forest green
background. - A strong silhouette that remains recognizable at small
sizes.

The geometric shape is part of the visual navigation system. Keep each
service's assigned shape consistent wherever that service appears
elsewhere on the website.

### Shared Icon Style Prompt

Append this shared style description to every individual icon prompt:

> Premium medieval heraldic emblem, dark fantasy guild identity,
> symmetrical composition, engraved antique metal relief, aged gold,
> bronze and subtle silver details, deep forest green background, strong
> readable silhouette, refined craftsmanship, restrained ornamentation,
> consistent visual style across the entire icon collection. Centered
> composition, square 1:1 canvas. No text, no letters, no numbers, no
> watermark.

### 3.1 Monster Elimination

**Geometric shape:** Circle\
**Central symbol:** A longsword piercing a monstrous fang.

**Meaning:** Completion, control, and the elimination of a direct
threat.

**Generation prompt:**

> A circular medieval heraldic emblem featuring a powerful longsword
> piercing a monstrous fang. Strong, balanced central composition, with
> the sword as the dominant vertical element. The fang should look like
> a trophy taken from a dangerous creature, not a human tooth. \[Append
> the Shared Icon Style Prompt.\]

### 3.2 Escort & Protection

**Geometric shape:** Square shield with softly rounded corners\
**Central symbol:** A sturdy shield surrounded by two crossed spears.

**Meaning:** Defense, safe passage, and reliability.

**Generation prompt:**

> A square shield-shaped medieval heraldic emblem with softly rounded
> corners, featuring a sturdy central shield surrounded by two crossed
> spears. The shield is the dominant element, with the spears forming a
> balanced protective frame. \[Append the Shared Icon Style Prompt.\]

### 3.3 Tracking & Investigation

**Geometric shape:** Upright triangle\
**Central symbol:** A beast paw print above three subtle tracking lines.

**Meaning:** Search, direction, observation, and pursuit.

**Generation prompt:**

> An upward-pointing triangular medieval heraldic emblem featuring a
> detailed beast paw print above three subtle tracking lines. The paw
> print must be immediately recognizable, with the lines suggesting a
> trail or direction of movement. \[Append the Shared Icon Style
> Prompt.\]

### 3.4 Relic Recovery

**Geometric shape:** Inverted triangle\
**Central symbol:** An ancient faceted gemstone or relic enclosed by
delicate metalwork.

**Meaning:** Discovery, ancient knowledge, and valuable recovery.

**Generation prompt:**

> An inverted triangular medieval heraldic emblem featuring an ancient
> mystical relic suspended in the center. The relic is a faceted
> gemstone enclosed by delicate ornamental metalwork. Keep the central
> object clear and recognizable, with restrained arcane details.
> \[Append the Shared Icon Style Prompt.\]

### 3.5 Emergency Response

**Geometric shape:** Diamond\
**Central symbol:** A burning signal beacon above a small watchtower.

**Meaning:** Urgency, warning, and rapid response.

**Generation prompt:**

> A diamond-shaped medieval heraldic emblem featuring a burning signal
> beacon above a small watchtower. Use a sharp, recognizable silhouette,
> with the flame clearly visible above the tower. The composition should
> communicate an urgent warning without looking aggressive or chaotic.
> \[Append the Shared Icon Style Prompt.\]

## 4. Icon Production Requirements

-   Generate each icon as a separate image.
-   Use a square 1:1 canvas for every icon.
-   Keep the emblem centered with comfortable spacing around its outer
    shape.
-   Keep the assigned geometric base shape clearly visible.
-   Use a consistent scale, level of detail, lighting direction, and
    material treatment across all five icons.
-   Prioritize silhouette clarity over tiny decorative details.
-   Ensure the icon remains recognizable when displayed at approximately
    64 × 64 pixels.
-   Do not include labels, typography, numbers, UI mockups, or
    additional objects outside the emblem.
-   Avoid changing the assigned geometric shape between generations.
-   The icons should look like parts of one commissioned heraldic
    collection, not five unrelated illustrations.

## 5. Design Consistency

The website's visual identity should remain consistent across: - The
main guild logo and favicon. - Service category icons. - Completed
contract illustrations. - Character and client portraits. - Buttons,
cards, borders, dividers, and status indicators.

Use the same core palette and restrained medieval heraldic language
throughout. Individual illustrations may introduce local environmental
colors, but the overall website interface should remain anchored in Deep
Forest, Guild Green, Antique Gold, and Old Parchment.
