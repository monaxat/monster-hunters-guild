# 2. Our Services

### Professional Solutions for Unnatural Threats

Every contract begins with understanding the threat. Every operation
ends with a clear result.

### Monster Elimination

We locate, assess, and eliminate dangerous creatures --- from
territorial predators to ancient beasts.

**Typical assignments:** Wyverns, trolls, ghouls, nest extermination,
creature containment.

### Caravan Protection

We provide experienced armed escorts for merchants, travelers, and
diplomatic parties crossing dangerous territory.

**Typical assignments:** Trade route escort, high-risk passage, secure
transport.

### Tracking & Investigation

When people disappear or unexplained attacks occur, our specialists find
the source and establish what happened.

**Typical assignments:** Missing persons, creature tracking,
investigation of unusual incidents.

### Territory Protection

We help settlements, estates, mines, and trade routes remain secure
against recurring threats.

**Typical assignments:** Perimeter patrols, threat assessment, long-term
protection contracts.

### Relic Recovery

We retrieve valuable objects from ruins, cursed sites, and other
locations where ordinary recovery teams cannot operate.

**Typical assignments:** Artifact retrieval, expedition security,
recovery and transport.

### Emergency Response

For urgent situations that cannot wait, we assemble and deploy an
appropriate response team.

**Typical assignments:** Active creature attacks, civilian evacuation,
containment of immediate threats.

## Proven in the Field

A selection of contracts completed by our hunters. Some details have
been withheld at the request of our clients.

#### Contract #0847 --- The Ashen Pass Incident

**Location:** Northern Territories\
**Threat Level:** High\
**Duration:** 3 days\
**Status:** Resolved

A wyvern had established a nesting ground near a major trade route.
Several travelers were reported missing, and caravan traffic had come to
a halt.

**Outcome:** The creature was eliminated. The route was inspected and
reopened.

#### Contract #0846 --- Silence Beneath the Abbey

**Location:** Westfall\
**Threat Level:** Critical\
**Duration:** 5 days\
**Status:** Resolved

The monks of an abandoned abbey reported strange sounds beneath the
crypt and the disappearance of two members of their order.

**Outcome:** The source of the disturbance was contained. Further
details remain confidential.

#### Contract #0845 --- The Hollowwood Disappearances

**Location:** Eastern Marches\
**Threat Level:** Medium\
**Duration:** 2 days\
**Status:** Resolved

Three hunters vanished while investigating an abandoned settlement near
the Hollowwood.

**Outcome:** The missing hunters were recovered. The threat responsible
for the disappearances was neutralized.

#### Contract #0844 --- The Blackwater Crossing

**Location:** Blackwater River\
**Threat Level:** Medium\
**Duration:** 6 days\
**Status:** Resolved

A merchant convoy had suffered repeated attacks while crossing the
river. The client requested a secure passage for three consecutive
shipments.

**Outcome:** All shipments reached their destination without loss.

#### Contract #0843 --- A Debt in Silver

**Location:** Greyhaven\
**Threat Level:** Low\
**Duration:** 1 day\
**Status:** Resolved

A creature had been entering a silver mine after nightfall, forcing
workers to abandon the lower tunnels.

**Outcome:** The tunnels were secured and mining operations resumed.
