# Main Logo — The Wyrm Hunters

A sophisticated heraldic emblem for an elite dark fantasy monster hunting guild named "The Wyrm Hunters".

The emblem is built around a perfectly circular medieval shield, not a triangular shield. At its center, a powerful serpentine wyrm is coiled and pierced through its body by a vertical three-pronged fantasy trident. The trident should resemble an ancient monster-hunting weapon, with elegant elongated prongs, not a real-world national symbol.

Behind the creature, at the lower part of the composition, a rugged land horizon stretches across the shield. A large rising sun emerges from behind the horizon, symbolizing the dawn after darkness and the protection of civilization.

The serpent and trident form the dominant central composition. The rising sun and landscape remain secondary decorative elements.

Visual style: premium medieval heraldry, dark fantasy engraving, intricate but controlled metallic ornamentation, refined antique gold and aged bronze details, deep forest green and nearly black background, subtle silver accents.

Strong symmetrical circular composition, recognizable silhouette, precise engraved linework, elegant negative space, timeless guild insignia.

The emblem should look like the official seal of an ancient, wealthy and highly respected monster hunting organization.

No text, no letters, no typography, no mockup, no merchandise, no surrounding objects. Centered composition, isolated emblem, square canvas.
