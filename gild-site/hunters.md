# The Hunters — Visual Portrait Descriptions

## Global Portrait Prompt

All images must use a square 1:1 aspect ratio.

Use this shared visual direction for every client portrait. Append the
individual character description after this block.

**Style**

Dark fantasy character portrait, painterly digital oil painting,
semi-realistic illustration, classical portraiture, rich but controlled
brushwork, detailed and expressive facial features, subtle atmospheric
lighting, natural skin texture, refined material rendering, cinematic
depth, sophisticated color grading.

The portraits should feel like a coherent collection commissioned by a
wealthy medieval guild. Each character should retain an individual
palette, mood, clothing style, and environment, while sharing the same
overall rendering quality and artistic treatment.

**Composition and framing**

-   Head-and-shoulders portrait, suitable for a small circular avatar.
-   Face clearly visible, centered, and occupying a substantial part of
    the image.
-   Character facing the viewer or turned slightly three-quarters toward
    the viewer.
-   Keep the head, hair, and important facial features safely inside the
    central area.
-   Shoulders may be partially visible; avoid full-body compositions.
-   Background should be softly blurred and atmospheric, supporting the
    character's identity without competing with the face.
-   Use a square 1:1 canvas, with enough space around the head for a
    circular crop.

**Consistency and restrictions**

-   Keep the character's age, gender presentation, profession, and
    defining features consistent with the individual description.
-   Give each client a distinct face and silhouette; do not reuse the
    same generic face.
-   Vary palettes and lighting according to the character, but preserve
    the shared painterly dark-fantasy style.
-   Avoid exaggerated cartoon features, anime styling, photorealistic
    photography, modern objects, modern clothing, and contemporary
    visual elements.
-   No text, lettering, captions, logos, watermarks, decorative frames,
    or circular borders.
-   Do not place important facial features or identifying accessories
    near the image edges.

## Sir Aldric Vane
**Role:** Guild Master

**Visual portrait description:**
A mature, broad-shouldered veteran warrior in his late fifties. He has
short, neatly slicked-back grey hair, a weathered face marked by deep
wrinkles, cold grey eyes, and a long healed scar running from his brow
down toward his jaw. Keep the scar clear and believable without
distorting his facial features.

He wears a high-collared, iron-grey cloak secured with a restrained
heraldic clasp, over well-maintained but visibly worn leather and steel
armor. His equipment is practical and of high quality, showing years of
service rather than decoration. A long sword is carried at his side.
Subtle scars on his hands and a composed, commanding posture reinforce
his experience.

Show him as a seasoned commander: controlled, authoritative, and
difficult to intimidate, not simply an angry or gloomy knight. A dim
guild armory or weapons hall with restrained firelight provides the
background.

## Lyra Moonshade
**Role:** Master Tracker

**Visual portrait description:**
A lean adult elf with long pointed ears, silver-green hair braided into
a low ponytail, and clear green eyes. Her expression is observant and
composed, suggesting someone who notices details others miss.

She wears practical forest-ranger leather gear designed for movement and
quiet travel, with a bow, quiver, and a sheathed dagger. Small copper
amulets and a few understated personal details add character without
making her outfit ornamental.

Her silhouette should be light, agile, and functional. Place her in a
misty woodland at morning, with soft natural light filtering through the
trees. Avoid an overly decorative or ceremonial elf design.

## Brom Ironbelly
**Role:** Chief Alchemist & Equipment Specialist

**Visual portrait description:**
A short, powerfully built dwarf with a massive torso, broad shoulders, a
prominent belly, and strong working hands. He has a thick reddish-grey
beard, shaved or closely cropped hair at the sides, a ruddy face, a
broken nose, and a hard but cheerful smile.

He wears a stained leather alchemist's apron over sturdy work clothes. A
heavy belt carries glass vials, small tools, and practical equipment. He
holds a bottle or alchemical flask, as if explaining one of his latest
mixtures.

His appearance should communicate practical expertise and an energetic
workshop personality. Use a cluttered but believable alchemical
laboratory as the background, with shelves, tools, glassware, and warm
workshop light. Make his dwarf proportions unmistakable; do not depict
him as a large human.

## Morgana Ashveil
**Role:** Arcane Specialist

**Visual portrait description:**
A pale, enigmatic adult woman with dark, ashen hair streaked with silver
and deep, almost black eyes. A faint violet glimmer may be visible in
her eyes, subtle enough to feel like a trace of magic rather than a
supernatural glow.

She wears a dark, hooded cloak with restrained runic details. A silver
amulet containing a dark stone rests at her chest. The clothing is
elegant but practical, with no excessive ornaments or stereotypical
witch accessories.

Use restrained magical accents: a faint violet reflection in her eyes, a
barely glowing amulet, or a few subtle runes catching the light. Avoid
large spell effects or dramatic magical explosions. The background may
contain indistinct arcane markings and flickering flames, balanced by
cool blue ambient light.

## Finn Blackwood
**Role:** Scout & Field Operative

**Visual portrait description:**
A young adult man, approximately 22--24 years old, lean and agile, and
the youngest member of the team. He has tousled chestnut hair, freckles,
lively eyes, and a confident, slightly mischievous smile. His face
should read clearly as an adult, not a child or teenager.

He wears lightweight scout equipment suited to moving quickly through
the wilderness: a practical hood, fitted leather protection, a short
sword or knife, and a few compact field tools. His gear is used but well
maintained.

Show him in a half-crouched, alert posture, as if he has just spotted
something ahead. His expression should be self-assured and quick-witted
rather than childish. A misty field at dawn provides the setting, with
subdued natural light.
